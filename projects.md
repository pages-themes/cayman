      <h2 style="margin-top: 0.5rem;">Humane Intelligence Bias Bounty — 1st Place (x2)</h2>
      <p>
        Evaluated bias and extremist propagation patterns in production AI systems as part of <a href="https://humane-intelligence.org/programs-services/bias-bounty/" target="_blank">Humane Intelligence's</a> Bias Bounty program. Won both Challenge #1 and #2.
      </p>
      <p>
        <strong>Approach:</strong> Designed systematic evaluation frameworks to probe LLM outputs for demographic bias, stereotyping patterns, and content that could amplify extremist narratives. This wasn't just prompt engineering — it was structured adversarial testing with reproducible methodology, documented failure modes, and actionable recommendations for model developers.
      </p>
      <p>
        <strong>Why it matters:</strong> Bias bounties are one of the most direct ways to improve AI safety before deployment. This work directly mirrors the red-teaming and evaluation processes used by frontier AI labs to ship safer models.
      </p>
